# Caffeine

Every caffeinated drink, when it was had and how much caffeine (mg). Home
shows today's total and the times; Ask Bo can answer "how late do I usually
have caffeine?" or "does a late Celsius go with a worse night?".

## How it's said

- "had a Celsius"
- "Pike K-Cup at 7"
- "Spark", "an Advocare Spark"
- "large cold brew from Starbucks", "a Diet Coke", "double espresso"

## Rules

- One entry per drink, at the time it was had ("at 7" moves it).
- `drink`: the drink as said, cleaned up ("Celsius", "Starbucks Pike K-Cup",
  "Advocare Spark", "Grande cold brew"). Two of something is two entries.
- `mg`: only when he says a number. Leave it out otherwise — the server fills
  it in from a table (his usual drinks) or an estimate, and says which in
  `estimate`.
- `notes`: anything else ("half a cup", "decaf mix").

## His usual drinks

- Starbucks Pike Place K-Cup ("Pike", "a K-Cup", "coffee" at home)
- Advocare Spark (a drink-mix stick)
- Celsius (a 12 oz can)
