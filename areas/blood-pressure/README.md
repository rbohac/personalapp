# Blood pressure

A home cuff reading: systolic over diastolic, usually with a pulse.

## How it's said

- "BP 120/80 pulse 72"
- "took my blood pressure, one twenty-eight over eighty-two, heart rate sixty-eight"
- "122 over 79, 70" — a third number after the reading is the pulse
- "blood pressure this morning 131/85 left arm"

## Rules

- The first number is always systolic, the second diastolic. If they come out
  reversed (second larger than first), swap them and say so in `note`.
- A lone third number right after a reading is the pulse.
- "Left"/"right" means the arm.
- Don't record a reading with only one number; leave it out and say why.
- For `note`: mention a reading at or above 180 systolic or 120 diastolic
  (worth acting on), nothing for ordinary readings.
