# Health events

One-off things that happened to the body: procedures, injections that
aren't on a schedule, a fall, a doctor's visit with something done, a fluid
draw. The point is a dated record that can be looked back on ("when did I
have the knee injection?").

## How it's said

- "got a stem cell injection in my right knee" → what "stem cell injection",
  where "right knee"
- "they drained 40 ml of fluid off my left knee" → what "fluid drained",
  where "left knee", amount "40 ml"
- "cortisone shot in my shoulder at Dr. Patel's" → provider "Dr. Patel"
- "rolled my ankle on the stairs"

## Rules

- `what` is a short name for what happened, in the owner's words.
- `where` is the body part with its side when said ("right knee").
- `amount` keeps its unit as said ("40 ml", "2 vials").
- Two things done in one visit ("injection, and they drained fluid first")
  are two entries, so each can be found on its own.
- Weekly shots and daily medications are not health events — they have
  their own areas.
