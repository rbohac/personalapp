# Medications

Pills and other daily medications taken. Injections go in **shots**.

## How it's said

- "took my metformin"
- "took my morning meds"
- "vitamin D 2000 IU"
- "ibuprofen 400 for my back"

## Rules

- One entry per medication named. "Took my morning meds" is one entry with
  medication "morning meds" (a group the owner takes together).
- Keep the dose as said, with its unit ("500 mg", "2 tablets").
- A reason ("for my back") goes in `notes`.

## Known medications

Add the ones you take, with their usual dose, so a name said by voice is
spelled right and a missing dose can be filled in:

- Vyvanse — mornings, skipped some days
- Morning vitamins — taken with the morning meds (list to come)
- Wolverine patch — a patch, on a short course (every other day, then
  weekly); "put on my Wolverine patch" is medication "Wolverine patch"
