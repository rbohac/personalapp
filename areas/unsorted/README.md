# Unsorted

Things to keep that no other area fits yet, so nothing said is lost. When
the same kind of thing keeps landing here, it gets an area of its own and
these are re-filed.

## Rules

- Use this **only** when no other area fits the reading or event.
- `text` is the words as said, unchanged.
- `about` is two or three words naming what kind of thing it is ("lab
  result", "pain level"), so similar ones can be grouped later.
