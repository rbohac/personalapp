# Household

Time spent keeping up the house: cleaning, tidying, laundry, repairs, yard
work. The house is on **Brock Road**; time there is the default. The goal is
at least 15 minutes a day, so the minutes are what matter.

## How it's said

- "cleaned the kitchen for 20 minutes"
- "half an hour of laundry and dishes"
- "spent an hour on the deck at Brock Road"
- "15 minutes tidying"

## Rules

- `minutes` is required. "Half an hour" is 30, "an hour" 60, "an hour and a
  half" 90. With no time said, don't guess — leave it missing so it's asked.
- `task` is a few words: "kitchen", "laundry and dishes", "deck repair".
- `place` is "Brock Road" unless another place is named.
- Several jobs in one sentence with one total time is one entry; separate
  times are separate entries.
